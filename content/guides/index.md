## Work with your notes {.cards}

- **Connect Claude to your Obsidian vault**

  Give Claude read access to a vault and check its answer against a note on disk.

  [Connect Claude to a vault](/guides/claude-obsidian/)

- **Export Readwise highlights to Markdown**

  Import saved highlights and find the quotation, comment and source link on disk.

  [Import Readwise highlights](/guides/readwise-markdown/)

- **Edit an Obsidian vault in your browser**

  Edit the body and properties of a note, then check the file in your vault.

  [Open a vault in Editor](/guides/obsidian-browser/)

- **Connect ChatGPT to your Markdown notes**

  Ask for notes that match a property and check the quoted text against the file.

  [Connect ChatGPT](/guides/chatgpt-markdown/)

- **Save Zotero annotations as Markdown notes**

  Bring a marked-up passage, your comment and the original attachment into the same collection.

  [Import Zotero annotations](/guides/zotero-annotations/)

- **Obsidian MCP servers compared**

  Choose where the server reads your notes and see which computer must stay online.

  [Compare local and hosted MCP](/guides/obsidian-mcp-servers/)
