## Which collections can be shared {#which}

Cloud copy and Private collections can both be shared. Sharing covers the whole collection; to share part of one, make a separate collection. Sharing is managed in the Connect workspace at [editor.mdbase.dev/connect](https://editor.mdbase.dev/connect). Open the collection and find **People & sharing**. Only the collection's owner sees it.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including inviting and removing people. |
| Editor | Edit notes, manage types, rename the collection, and connect apps. |
| Viewer | Read the collection and connect read-only apps. |

Each member’s devices keep a full copy of the collection, like yours. A viewer who opens the collection in mdbase Editor sees it read-only.

## Invite someone {#invite}

1. Choose **Invite person**.
2. Under **Invite using**, choose **Verified email** and enter their email address, or choose **Sharing code** and enter the code they gave you.
3. Choose **Viewer** or **Editor**, then **Create invitation**.
4. Copy the invitation link and send it to them yourself.

Email invitations only work for someone who already has a Connect account with that email address verified. If they signed up after you created the invitation, create a new one.

## Sharing codes {#code}

Use a sharing code when you cannot invite someone by email, for example if they sign in with a different address. The person you are inviting creates the code on the **Account & sessions** page in the Connect workspace, under **Sharing code** → **Generate code**, and sends it to you. A code can be used once. Invitations and sharing codes expire after seven days.

## Accept an invitation {#accept}

Open the invitation link while signed in to the account it was created for. Connect shows **A collection was shared with you**. Choose **Accept** to add the collection to your collections, then open it in the Editor or approve other apps to use it.

In a Private collection, an owner or editor approves the new member’s first device. You and the new member compare a six-digit code, in person or on a call, before that device gets the collection’s keys.

## Change or remove access {#manage}

In **People & sharing**, change a person's role from the list, or choose **Remove**. Removing someone also revokes their devices and the apps they connected to the collection. They keep the files they already had, but get no further changes. Invitations that have not been accepted are listed under **Pending invitations**, where you can cancel them.
